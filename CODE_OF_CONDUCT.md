# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- being respectful and kind
- giving and receiving constructive feedback
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- inviting and valuing diverse perspectives

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments
- trolling, intimidation, or repeated disruption
- publishing private information without permission
- behavior inappropriate in a professional environment

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the repository maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly and fairly.
